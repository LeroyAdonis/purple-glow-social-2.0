Both, reversed on the brand fielddoodle top, Lucide bottom

What I can see from the files: the doodles are filled paths, not strokes — their line weight is baked into the shapes, so it cannot be tuned to match anything else, and 451 of them are 451 different hands. They are excellent at 32px and up and they are delightful as illustration. At 16px the wobble becomes noise, which is why I would keep them out of the sidebar, toolbar and tables. The pack does include the platform marks (instagram, facebook, linkedin, whatsapp, tiktok), which solves sourcing — though the trademarks still belong to their owners and official assets take priority.
