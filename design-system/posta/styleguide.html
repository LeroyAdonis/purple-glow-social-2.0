The gold rules

  • Ink on gold 8.63:1 — the primary button, that is the job
  • Gold on clay 3.63:1 — large text or graphics only
  • Gold on paper 1.91:1 — never text, hairline or focus ring
  • One gold element per screen. Two means do neither

Corrected in this version

  • Warning was #B5761A at 3.51:1 on paper — failed AA, now #8A5A12 at 5.50:1
  • Focus rings are per surface: clay on light (6.94:1), cream on clay (6.45:1)
  • Gold as a focus ring was invisible on paper, which is why it is banned

CC0, 451 SVGs. Charming and hand-drawn, which is the brand voice. They are filled paths, not strokes: measured at 24px they go chunky, and at 16px the calendar becomes a square and the gear becomes a lump. So they illustrate, they never operate the UI.

Never

  • An emoji used as an icon
  • A doodle in a toolbar or a table
  • A stock speech bubble as the logo
  • Two stroke weights on one screen

Always

  • Lucide under 32px, doodles at 32px and above
  • An icon with a label, or an aria-label
  • Official brand marks for Instagram, Facebook, X and LinkedIn
  • One icon weight per view

Voice, and the rules that override it

Warm, direct, competent. Never performing.

How it sounds

  • Speaks to you, not “users”
  • SA English: organise, colour, centre
  • Rands as R299. Times as 17:30 SAST, 24-hour
  • Language names in their own language: isiZulu, Sesotho, Afrikaans
  • “No posts scheduled yet. Pick a day and Posta will draft three.”

Banned words

  • oops, whoops, simply, just, easily
  • revolutionary, game-changing, seamless, effortless, unlock

Honesty rules — these override tone

  • Never claim a number we cannot show. The old front page said “5,000+ SA businesses” with zero customers. It is gone.
  • Never invent a testimonial. Three were live from demo personas. Gone.
  • Never claim a capability that is not built. “Coming soon” is honest.
  • Name the limit: “Free plan: 10 credits, 5 scheduled posts.”
  • Errors state the cause and the fix. Never “something went wrong”.
  • Do not claim load-shedding resilience. Eskom reported 500+ days without load-shedding on 2 Oct 2026.
