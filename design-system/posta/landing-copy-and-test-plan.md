# Posta landing page — copy, image and test plan (2026-10-27)

Built from the marketing skills: `copywriting`, `copy-editing`, `image`, `ad-creative`, `ab-testing`.
This is the non-code half of the Cohere restyle.

## The one action

Start a free account. Every other link on the page is secondary and styled as a ghost so it cannot compete.

## Headline angles, in the order I would test them

1. **Outcome, plain** — "Social posts, written for you." (live now)
2. **Time saved** — "Your week of posts, written in one sitting."
3. **Language, which nobody else does** — "Your posts, in all 11 South African languages."
4. **Control** — "Nothing publishes until you say yes."

Angle 1 is live because it states the product in six words with no jargon; 3 is the real differentiator
and is the one I expect to win once there is traffic to measure it.

## A/B test to run first

- **Hypothesis:** naming the 11 languages in the headline raises signup rate, because no competitor
  offers it and it is checkable.
- **Variants:** A = "Social posts, written for you." · B = "Your posts, in all 11 South African languages."
- **Primary metric:** signup started. **Guardrail:** bounce on the landing page.
- **Assignment:** sticky per visitor (cookie), 50/50, held for at least two full weeks and a minimum
  sample of ~1,100 visitors per arm for a 5pp lift at 80% power.
- **Rule:** do not peek and stop early; a p-value read before the fixed horizon is not a result.

## Copy-editing pass applied

- Removed the adjective stacking ("AI-powered", "seamless") — nothing on this page claims a capability
  that cannot be demonstrated in the product.
- One voice, first person plural nowhere: the page speaks to "you" and about Posta, never "we".
- Every feature line now names the benefit in the same sentence.
- No invented numbers, customers or testimonials. Prices (R0 / R299 / R999) and the 11 languages are the
  only numbers on the page, and both are real.

## Image direction (from `image`)

The photographs are documentary South African small-business pictures with real credits, not stock
aspiration. Direction for the next pass: one subject with a face near frame centre, shot at eye level,
with room in the negative space for type. The bakehouse interior currently in the hero is close; a
portrait of an owner at their counter would be better and is worth sourcing properly.
