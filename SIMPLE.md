# Purple Glow Social — Plain English Guide

## What is Purple Glow Social?

Purple Glow Social is a tool that **writes your social media posts for you**.

You know how running a business means you're already busy enough — finding time to post on Instagram, Facebook, Twitter and LinkedIn feels like a full-time job on its own?

Purple Glow does the hard part. You tell it about your business, and it writes posts your customers will love. In **all 11 South African languages**.

- **Small business owners** who know they need social media but don't have time
- **Township entrepreneurs** who want to reach customers in their home language
- **Startups** that need to look professional online without hiring a marketing person
- **Anyone** who's tired of staring at a blank screen trying to think of what to post

## How it works — really simply

### Step 1: Tell us about your business
What do you sell? Who are your customers? What's your vibe? Set it up once.

### Step 2: AI writes your posts
Our AI creates posts for Instagram, Facebook, Twitter and LinkedIn. In isiZulu, Afrikaans, English — any of the 11 languages. It sounds like you, not a robot.

### Step 3: You approve (or tweak)
Look things over. Make small changes if you want. Then schedule everything to post automatically.

### Step 4: Go back to running your business
That's it. Purple Glow posts for you at the best times. You don't have to think about it.
